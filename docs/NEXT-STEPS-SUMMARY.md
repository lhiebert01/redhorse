## Key Insights from Viral AI App Case Studies

### Lensa AI "Magic Avatars" Success Story

- Generated **$50M+ revenue** from a single feature
- Peak earnings: **$8M per day** during viral height
- Cost per avatar: ~50¢ → Price: $3.99+ = **87% margin**
- Source: https://startupspells.com/p/lensa-ai-avatars-riding-ai-wave

### What Made It Viral

1. **Perfect Timing** - Launched during peak AI interest
2. **Influencer Seeding** - Gave celebrities free avatars → unpaid promotion
3. **FOMO Loop** - Seeing friends' unique results created urgency
4. **Identity Hook** - "This is MY avatar" drives sharing
