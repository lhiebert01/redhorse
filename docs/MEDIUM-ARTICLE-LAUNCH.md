## What is Fire Horse Trivia?

It's a Kahoot-style multiplayer trivia game designed for parties and gatherings.

**The basics:**
- 400 expert questions across 16 categories
- 2-20 players compete simultaneously
- Everyone plays on their own phone
- Real-time scoring and live leaderboard
- No app download required — works instantly in any browser

**The categories include:**
- Fire Horse & The Great Race
- CNY Traditions & Food
- The Five Elements
- Zodiac Compatibility
- Historical War Horses
- Pop Culture & Modern Horses
- And 10 more...

**The idea:** Host it before Super Bowl kickoff, during a CNY dinner, or at any gathering where you want 20-30 minutes of competitive fun.

## Why the Fire Horse?

The Fire Horse (火馬) is one of the most powerful — and controversial — signs in Chinese astrology.

People born in Fire Horse years are said to be:
- Fiercely independent
- Unstoppable when pursuing goals
- Passionate and energetic
- Sometimes too willful for their own good

Famous Fire Horses (born 1966):
- **Halle Berry** — Oscar-winning actress
- **Janet Jackson** — Music icon
- **Adam Sandler** — Comedy legend
- **Mike Tyson** — Boxing champion

The Fire Horse only appears when the Horse zodiac year (every 12 years) aligns with the Fire element (every 10 years). That's why it takes 60 years for the cycle to repeat.

Fire Horse Trivia is part of a larger project I've been building: **Red Horse Oracle**.

- **Wealth Mode:** 6 lucky numbers (perfect for Super Bowl bets!)

Every Oracle is limited edition — only 888 will ever be minted for each zodiac sign. Like fine art prints, they're numbered and authenticated.

## How It Works

**For hosts:**
1. Purchase a pass ($4.88-$14.88 depending on how many games you want)
2. Get a 6-character party code
3. Share the code with your guests
4. Control the game from your phone or display on a TV

**For players:**
1. Go to redhorseoracle.com/party/join
2. Enter the party code
3. Choose a nickname and birth year (for zodiac)
4. Answer questions on your phone

**Scoring:**
- 100 points for correct answers
- Speed bonus for fast answers
- Streak bonus for consecutive correct answers
- Zodiac leaderboard shows which animal signs are winning

## The Technical Build

For the developers reading this:

- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 (App Router)
- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL)
- **Real-time:** Supabase Realtime for live syncing
- **Payments:** Stripe
- **AI (for Oracle):** Google Gemini 3 Pro
- **Hosting:** Vercel

The real-time sync was the trickiest part. Getting 20 phones to show the same question simultaneously, accept answers, and update the leaderboard without lag required careful architecture.
