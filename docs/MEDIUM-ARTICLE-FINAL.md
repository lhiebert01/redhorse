Here's what happens when you use Red Horse Oracle:

✅ Step-by-step flow
🎂 You enter your birth date
🧮 The system calculates your Chinese zodiac (example: Earth Dragon)
🗑️ Your birth date is immediately deleted
❌ not stored
❌ not logged
❌ not retained
🐲 Only your zodiac sign is kept (non-PII)
🤖🎨 AI generates your prophecy + artwork
🎁 You receive something beautiful that's safe to share publicly

That's it.

🚫 No account creation
🚫 No email harvesting
🚫 No tracking cookies
🚫 No analytics pixels
🚫 No Facebook Pixel shadowing your clickstream

Your zodiac sign (like Metal Rat or Fire Horse) can't identify you. Millions of people share each sign — like saying: "I'm a Sagittarius." ♐

## 🌍 Why This Matters

Most AI companies treat your data as the product.

You're not the customer.
You're the inventory. 🧾🧍‍♂️

But for a zodiac experience, I only need to calculate your sign.

I don't need to store your birth date.

So I don't. ✅

🧠 Privacy by Design = one question, repeated relentlessly:
"Do we actually need to keep this data?"

Most of the time, the answer is no. 🚫
And "no" is a powerful architecture decision. 🔥

🔒 Privacy-by-Design Rule: If the product doesn't need the data, don't keep it.
