## Why 8 Is the Luckiest Number in Chinese Culture

It comes down to how the number sounds.

In Mandarin Chinese:
- **八 (bā)** = the number 8
- **發 (fā)** = to prosper, to generate wealth

They sound almost identical.

So when Chinese speakers hear "8," they subconsciously hear "prosperity."

**888 = 發發發 = "Prosper, prosper, prosper!"**

| Number | Chinese | Sounds Like | Meaning |
|--------|---------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | 一 (yī) | — | Unity, first |
| 2 | 二 (èr) | — | Pairs, balance |
| 4 | 四 (sì) | 死 (sǐ) | ⚠️ DEATH (avoided!) |
| 6 | 六 (liù) | 流 (liú) | Flow, smooth |
| 8 | 八 (bā) | 發 (fā) | **PROSPERITY** |
| 9 | 九 (jiǔ) | 久 (jiǔ) | Longevity |
