## Long Version (Blog Post, Hacker News "Show HN")

### Show HN: Red Horse Oracle — Privacy-First Chinese Zodiac with AI Art & Multiplayer Trivia

I've been building Red Horse Oracle for the Year of the Fire Horse 2026, and I'm excited to share it with you.

#### The Opportunity

The Fire Horse (丙午年) comes only once every 60 years. Last time: 1966. Next time: 2086. That's a once-in-a-lifetime window — I wanted to create something memorable for it.

#### What I Built

**1. AI-Powered Oracle ($8.88)**

Enter your birth date, choose a path (Wealth, Power, Love, or Shield), and receive:
- A personalized prophecy generated by Gemini 3 Pro
- A unique talisman artwork in traditional Chinese styles (Dunhuang, Song Dynasty, Tang Dynasty, Ink Wash)
- Limited edition numbering (only 888 per zodiac sign)
- Maker's Mark and Certificate of Authenticity

**2. Fire Horse Trivia Party Game ($2.88-$14.88)**

Host real-time trivia competitions for your CNY celebrations:
- 400 questions across 16 categories
- 2-20 players competing simultaneously
- Speed bonuses and streak bonuses
- Live leaderboards
- Host controls the pace (perfect for dinner parties)

#### The Privacy-First Architecture

Here's what makes this different: **Privacy by Design**.

I don't store your birth date. Ever.

The flow:
1. You enter your birth date
2. I calculate your Chinese zodiac sign
3. I immediately discard your birth date
4. Your oracle contains only your zodiac sign (not PII)

No accounts. No emails. No tracking. Your oracle is safe to share publicly because it contains no personally identifiable information.

In an age of data harvesting, I wanted to prove you can create something personalized AND respect privacy.

#### Technical Details

**Stack:**
- Next.js 14 (App Router) + TypeScript
- Supabase PostgreSQL + Realtime (for multiplayer)
- Stripe (payments)
- Google Gemini 3 Pro (text generation)
- Google Gemini 3 Pro Image (artwork generation)
- Vercel (hosting)

**Key Patterns:**
- useRef for mutable data in React callbacks (prevents stale closures)
- Game ID isolation for multi-tenant database queries
- Pre-generated question sets at purchase time
- Service role API routes for RLS bypass

The multiplayer game uses Supabase Realtime for broadcasting events (new question, answer stats, leaderboard updates). The host controls the game flow, players just answer on their phones.

#### Revenue Model

- Oracle: $8.88 (triple-8 for luck)
- Trivia passes: $2.88 (solo), $4.88 (day), $8.88 (weekend), $14.88 (festival)
- Limited editions create natural scarcity (888 per sign = 10,656 max)
- Cost per oracle: ~$0.71 (Gemini + Stripe fees)
- Margin: ~92%

#### What I Learned

1. **AI image generation is expensive** — Gemini 3 Pro Image costs $0.134/image. That's real money at scale.

2. **Privacy can be a feature** — "We don't store your data" is a genuine differentiator.

3. **60-year events create natural FOMO** — The Fire Horse timing is marketing gold.

4. **useRef saves lives** — React stale closures in real-time apps will destroy you. Use refs.

5. **Pre-generate everything** — Don't generate questions at game start. Generate at purchase time.
