### 3. PATENT Considerations

**Potentially Patentable Elements:**

| Innovation | Type | Feasibility |
|------------|------|-------------|
| Privacy-by-design AI generation pipeline | Utility Patent | MEDIUM |
| Watermarking system for authenticated vs shareable images | Utility Patent | MEDIUM |
| Certificate/Edition numbering system for digital art | Utility Patent | LOW |
| UI/UX for zodiac oracle experience | Design Patent | LOW |

**Patent Reality Check:**
- Utility patents cost $10,000-$25,000+ and take 2-3 years
- Software patents are harder to enforce post-Alice decision
- Trade secrets may be more practical for the algorithm

**Recommended Approach:**
1. **Document as Trade Secrets** - Keep prompt engineering confidential
2. **Consider Provisional Patent** - $1,500-3,000, buys 12 months to decide
3. **Focus on Trademarks** - More practical protection for branding
