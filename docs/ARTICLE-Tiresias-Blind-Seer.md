# The Blind Seer Who Saw Furthest

## An Ancient Greek Legend. A Modern Mission. And Why 20% of Every Oracle Supports the Blind.

In ancient Greece, there lived a prophet named **Tiresias**.

He was blind.

And yet, the Greeks believed he saw further than any mortal who ever lived.

Kings consulted him. Heroes sought his wisdom. The gods themselves respected his sight — not the sight of his eyes, but the sight of his soul.

**Tiresias could not see the world. But he could see the future.**

---

## The Paradox of Inner Vision

The Greeks had a profound belief:

> *Those without physical sight possess the greatest inner vision.*

This wasn't superstition. It was philosophy.

When you cannot see the distractions of the physical world — the flash of wealth, the shimmer of beauty, the chaos of movement — your mind turns inward. You perceive what others miss.

Tiresias wasn't blind *despite* being a great seer. He was a great seer *because* he was blind.

His blindness was his gift.

---

## The Oracle of Delphi

The ancient Greeks treasured their Oracle at Delphi above all else.

People traveled from across the Mediterranean — from Egypt, from Persia, from the edges of the known world — to ask the Oracle a single question.

What did they seek?

- 🏥 **Health** — Will I recover? Will my family be well?
- 💰 **Wealth** — Will my venture prosper? Should I take this risk?
- ❤️ **Love** — Who should I marry? Will this union be blessed?
- 🛡️ **Protection** — Is my city safe? What dangers approach?

These are the same questions humans have asked for millennia. The same questions we still ask today.

## Sight Is the Most Precious Gift

Here's what struck me when building this project:

**Sight.**

We use the word constantly:
- "I see what you mean"
- "She has great vision"
- "He's a visionary"
- "Can you see a path forward?"

Sight = Vision = Ability to see = Ability to imagine a future.

For those of us with functioning eyes, we take this for granted. We wake up, we see, we don't think about it.

But **7.6 million Americans** live with significant vision loss. Many cannot see the faces of their loved ones. Cannot read a book. Cannot watch a sunset.

Cannot see their future.
